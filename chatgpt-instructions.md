# Skein to Mood — ChatGPT Context

I am building a personal yarn mood journal and knit planning closet called **Skein to Mood**.

## Concept

Skein to Mood is a visual yarn closet and knit planning journal.

It tracks my growing yarn stash like a virtual closet, turns each yarn’s color and fiber into moodboards, and helps me find patterns that match each skein’s material, texture, color, and mood.

Korean description:

뉴욕에서 우연히 시작한 뜨개가 목도리, 넥워머, 가방, 버킷햇, 니트모자로 이어졌고, 어느새 손염색실을 모으는 무서운 길에 들어섰다.

Skein to Mood는 쌓여가는 실을 가상 클로젯처럼 기록하고, 각 실이 가진 색과 재질을 무드보드로 풀어내며, 그 실에 어울리는 패턴을 찾아가는 니트 플래닝 저널.

## Planning Preference

When suggesting knitting or crochet projects:
- Start from yarn first.
- Consider fiber, drape, weight, halo, and color mood.
- Suggest beginner-to-intermediate friendly ideas when possible.
- Prioritize wearable practicality and visual mood.
- Help connect yarns to patterns, moodboards, and project plans.
